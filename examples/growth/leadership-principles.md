# Leadership Principles

> Source: [MongoDB's five Leadership Principles](https://www.mongodb.com/company/leadership-principles)

These five principles are the evaluation framework used in P&G reviews, growth notes, and bragsheet entries across all growth tools.

## The Five Principles

- **Act Like Owners** — Take responsibility end-to-end; act in the long-term interest of the company. Most common signal types: cross-team enablement, operational excellence.
- **Communicate with Clarity** — Written and spoken communication is precise, structured, and audience-aware. Most common signal types: external storytelling, technical authority.
- **Focus on Outcomes** — Prioritize impact over activity; define success before starting work. Most common signal types: cross-team enablement, measurable change in how others work.
- **Never Stop Learning** — Seek feedback, reflect, and continuously improve skills and knowledge. Most common signal types: mentorship, personal cycle notes, acting on feedback visibly.
- **Build Extraordinary Teams** — Hire and develop the best; create an inclusive environment where people thrive. Most common signal types: mentorship, raising capability of people around you.

## How These Map to Impact Signals

| Principle | Signal Types | What to Look For |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Act Like Owners | `cross-team`, `ops-excellence` | End-to-end responsibility across team boundaries |
| Communicate with Clarity | `external`, `tech-authority` | Precise artifacts others can act on without follow-up |
| Focus on Outcomes | `cross-team`, `ops-excellence` | Measurable change in how others work, not just activity |
| Never Stop Learning | `mentorship`, personal notes | Seeking feedback and acting on it visibly |
| Build Extraordinary Teams | `mentorship`, `cross-team` | Raising the capability of people around you |
